Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Ucyaegis Training ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use and protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, username, email address and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form).
  • Learning activity: courses you enroll in, lectures you complete, assignment answers, questions you ask, and certificates issued to you.
  • Payment details: the amount, course and Transaction ID of your payment. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we do not store your card, bank or UPI credentials.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and give you access to courses.
  • To track your progress and issue and verify certificates.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that are needed to run the platform (for example, our payment gateway Razorpay) or when required by law. A certificate's Credential ID, course name and holder name can be checked by anyone using our "Verify Certificate" page.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and to keep the website secure (session and CSRF cookies). We do not use advertising cookies.

5. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your choices & deleting your account

You can update your profile at any time. Students can permanently delete their account from the Profile page; this removes your account, progress, submissions and certificates.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be shown on this page.

Questions about your data? Contact us.
