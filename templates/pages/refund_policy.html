Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Thank you for learning with Ucyaegis Training. Our courses are digital, pre-recorded learning content and access is given immediately after successful payment. Please read this policy before purchasing a course.

1. Digital content

Because course videos and resources become available as soon as you enroll, payments for courses are generally non-refundable once access has been granted.

2. When a refund can be requested

  • You were charged more than once for the same course (duplicate payment).
  • Your payment was deducted but you were not enrolled in the course.
  • A technical problem on our side prevented you from accessing the course and we were unable to resolve it.

3. When a refund is not available

  • You changed your mind after accessing lectures or resources.
  • You completed the course or a certificate has been issued.
  • Your account was suspended for violating our Terms & Conditions.
  • The course was free.

4. How to request a refund

Contact us through ucyaegis.pro within 7 days of the payment. Please include your registered username, the course name and your Transaction ID.

5. Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. It usually takes 5–7 working days for the amount to reflect, depending on your bank or UPI app.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Questions? Contact us.
